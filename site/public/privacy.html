Privacy — PersonalMoney

Last updated: 15 October 2026

PersonalMoney is software you run yourself. This page says what that means for your data.

The project collects nothing

This website and the project behind it do not collect, receive or store your financial data, your account details or your usage. There is no hosted PersonalMoney service to sign up to, and no analytics or advertising on this page. The one third party it involves is Google Fonts: the page loads its typeface (Inter) from there, so your browser contacts Google when you open it and Google sees your IP address.

Whoever runs an instance controls its data

Your accounts, transactions, categories and bank connections live in the database of the server you install PersonalMoney on. The person who runs that server — you, or whoever invited you to theirs — can see and back up that data, decides who may register, and is responsible for securing it. If you use somebody else's instance, ask them how they handle it.

The Android app

  • It talks only to the server address you enter when you sign in.
  • The one exception is the public Frankfurter exchange-rate API, which receives currency codes only — never amounts or identifiers.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics.

Receipt photos

Today, receipts are read on your device and the photo is discarded once it has been read; nothing is uploaded. When AI-assisted receipt reading arrives, a photo is sent only to the AI provider that the owner of your instance has configured, and to nobody else.
