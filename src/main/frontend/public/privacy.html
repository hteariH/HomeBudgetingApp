Privacy — PersonalMoney

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This PersonalMoney instance is run by its owner, not by the PersonalMoney project. Your data is stored on their server, and they are responsible for it. PersonalMoney is open-source software that anyone can run; the project itself never receives the data of an instance it does not operate. Ask the owner of this instance if you want to know who they are or how they look after the server.

In short

  • Your data lives in the database of this instance's server. Its owner can see and back it up.
  • The software contacts other services only for exchange rates, the web app's font and, if you connect them, your bank or HomeMoney.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers.
  • You can export or delete your data yourself, in the app's settings.

The Android app also keeps a copy of your ledger on the phone so it works without a network, and stores its sign-in tokens in Android's encrypted preferences.

2. What it sends elsewhere

  • Exchange rates. The server and the Android app ask the public Frankfurter API for rates. Only currency codes are sent, never amounts or identifiers.
  • Your bank. If you connect a bank (for example Monobank), the server calls that bank's API with the credentials you gave it, to fetch your statements.
  • HomeMoney. If you import from HomeMoney, the server calls HomeMoney's API with the consent you grant there.
  • Mail. If the owner has configured an SMTP server, address checks and password resets are sent through it, so that mail provider sees your email address.
  • Fonts. The web app loads its typeface (Inter) from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when you open it, and Google sees your IP address. The Android app contains its fonts and does not.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers, in the web app or the Android app.

3. Receipt photos

Receipts are read on your device and the photo is discarded once it has been read; nothing is uploaded.

4. Your choices

  • Export a full copy of your data from the app's settings.
  • Delete your account from the app's settings. Everything belonging to it, including bank credentials, is erased from the server and cannot be recovered. Backups the owner made earlier may still hold a copy until they are rotated out.
  • For anything else, such as access, correction or how long backups are kept, contact the owner of this instance.

5. Changes

The owner may change how they run this instance. The software's own behaviour is described by its public source code.
