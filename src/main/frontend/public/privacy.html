Privacy Policy — PersonalMoney

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the PersonalMoney Android application and the PersonalMoney service at personalmoney.app (together, the Service) handle your information.

The Service is operated by mamoru (“we”, “us”, “our”). Contact: aberkunsky@gmail.com.

In short

  • PersonalMoney is an account-based service. You register with an email address and a name, and your accounts, transactions, categories and subscriptions are stored on our server so the same data reaches every device you sign in on.
  • The app is offline-first: everything is kept in a local database on your device and works with no network, then synchronises when there is one.
  • If you connect a bank, the credentials you give us are encrypted and stored on the server only. They are never sent back to a device.
  • We show ads through Google AdMob and sell an optional subscription through Google Play Billing.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, permanently.

3. What stays on your device and never reaches us

  • Receipt photos. Scanning a receipt opens the system camera, recognises the text on-device with Tesseract (the language data ships inside the app), and deletes the image from the cache as soon as it has been read. The app declares no camera permission and reads no photo library. No image, and no text extracted from one, is ever uploaded.
  • Biometric unlock. Handled entirely by Android. We never see a fingerprint or a face.
  • Your theme, language and app preferences.
  • Exported backup files. You choose where they are written through the system file picker; they do not pass through us.

4. Third parties

4.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The app shows banner ads. Google may collect the data listed in section 2 to serve and measure them. You can opt out of personalised advertising through the in-app consent dialog (shown where required, e.g. in the EEA and the UK) or in Android Settings → Google → Ads. See Google’s partner-sites notice.

4.2 Google Play Billing (payments)

Premium is sold through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details — Google processes the payment and tells us only that a purchase exists and whether it is still active.

4.3 Resend (transactional email)

Address-confirmation and password-reset mail is sent through Resend, which therefore processes your email address.

4.4 Banks and aggregators you choose to connect

If you connect Monobank or HomeMoney, we call their APIs on your behalf with the credentials you supplied, and import the transactions they return. We ask them for nothing beyond statement data. Disconnecting a connection in the app deletes the stored credential.

4.5 Exchange rates

Rates come from the European Central Bank via the public Frankfurter API and from our own server. Only currency codes are sent — never amounts, never identifiers.

5. Where the data lives

On a virtual server in the European Union, in a PostgreSQL database, behind HTTPS. All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted in transit. Bank credentials are additionally encrypted at rest with a key held only on that server.

6. How long we keep it

  • Your content, for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted account → everything belonging to it is erased immediately and irreversibly, including bank credentials. There is no recovery.
  • Server access logs, a short rolling window.
  • Purchase records are retained where tax or accounting law requires it.

7. Your rights and how to use them

  • Delete your account — in the app, Settings → Delete account, or on the web at personalmoney.app/app, or by emailing aberkunsky@gmail.com.
  • Export your data — Settings → Export writes a full JSON backup.
  • Access or correct your data — everything we hold about you is visible in the app; write to us if something is missing.
  • If you are in the EEA or the UK, the GDPR applies. Our legal bases are performance of a contract (running the service you signed up for), consent (personalised advertising), and legitimate interest (security and abuse prevention). You may complain to your national data protection authority.

8. Children

PersonalMoney is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the local equivalent). If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed, bank credentials are encrypted at rest, transport is HTTPS only, sessions expire and can be revoked, and the login endpoint is rate-limited. No system is perfect; if you find a problem, please report it to aberkunsky@gmail.com.

10. Changes

We will update the date at the top when this policy changes, and announce material changes in the app.
